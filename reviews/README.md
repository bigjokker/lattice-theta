# Local review evidence

These documents record AI-assisted mathematical audits by Grok and Astra.
They are included for transparency about proof checking and revisions, and
are not presented as journal peer review or independent published research.
The manuscripts, exact certificates, and reproducible verifier outputs carry
the claims and can be checked without trusting the reviewers.

The [focused rank-four review](rank-four-counterexample-review.md) accepted
the all-level coset bijection, normalization, complete 315-point enumeration,
Aut(G)={I,-I}, and epsilon zero on both elements. Its scope is this
counterexample; it does not re-review every earlier result or establish
minimum determinant or literature priority.

The remaining reviews cover the lower-rank proofs, source scope, modularity,
finite domain, and later-shell regressions. Their chronological observations
are retained as audit history. Handoff prompts and assignments are excluded.
